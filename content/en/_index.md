<h2 class="tcm-section-title">What is MeshCore?</h2>
<p class="tcm-section-sub">MeshCore is open-source firmware that turns small LoRa radios into an off-grid messaging network. Messages hop between radios across miles of Appalachian terrain, so the network keeps working when the power or the towers don't.</p>

<div class="tcm-grid">
  <div class="tcm-card">
    <div class="tcm-icon"><i class="fas fa-bolt"></i></div>
    <h3>Works when nothing else does</h3>
    <p>No cell towers, internet, or accounts. If the radios have power, messages get through.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tcm-card">
    <div class="tcm-icon"><i class="fas fa-route"></i></div>
    <h3>Smarter routing</h3>
    <p>Fixed <strong>repeaters</strong> carry the traffic. Phones and handhelds don't rebroadcast everything, so the mesh stays quiet and scales as it grows.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tcm-card">
    <div class="tcm-icon"><i class="fas fa-lock"></i></div>
    <h3>Private by default</h3>
    <p>Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted. Public and private channels are there for group chat.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tcm-card">
    <div class="tcm-icon"><i class="fas fa-dollar-sign"></i></div>
    <h3>Cheap to start</h3>
    <p>A starter radio is about <strong>$20–40</strong>. Flash it from your browser, pair it with your phone, and you're on.</p>
  </div>
</div>
{{< /blocks/section >}}

<h2 class="tcm-section-title">How the mesh fits together</h2>
<p class="tcm-section-sub">MeshCore radios each have a job. Most people only need a companion, but every repeater on a high spot makes the whole network better.</p>

<div class="tcm-grid">
  <div class="tcm-card">
    <div class="tcm-icon"><i class="fas fa-mobile-screen"></i></div>
    <h3>Companion</h3>
    <p>Your personal radio. It pairs with the MeshCore app on your phone or computer over Bluetooth or USB so you can send messages.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tcm-card">
    <div class="tcm-icon"><i class="fas fa-tower-broadcast"></i></div>
    <h3>Repeater</h3>
    <p>An always-on relay on a roof, tower, or ridge. Repeaters are the backbone that carries messages across the Tri-Cities.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tcm-card">
    <div class="tcm-icon"><i class="fas fa-comments"></i></div>
    <h3>Room Server</h3>
    <p>A small bulletin board on the mesh. It stores posts so people can catch up on what they missed while they were offline.</p>
  </div>
</div>
{{< /blocks/section >}}

{{< blocks/section color="white" >}}
<h2 class="tcm-section-title">Get on the air in four steps</h2>
<p class="tcm-section-sub">Most people are sending their first message within an hour.</p>

<div class="tcm-grid tcm-steps">
  <div class="tcm-card">
    <h3>Pick a radio</h3>
    <p>Heltec V3/V4, RAK WisMesh, Seeed, or LilyGO. Plenty of supported options at around $20–40.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tcm-card">
    <h3>Flash MeshCore</h3>
    <p>Plug in over USB and use the <a href="https://flasher.meshcore.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">web flasher</a>. Nothing to install.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tcm-card">
    <h3>Set the US preset</h3>
    <p>In the app, choose <strong>USA/Canada (Recommended)</strong> so you can hear the local mesh.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tcm-card">
    <h3>Say hello</h3>
    <p>Send an advert, check the Public channel, and see which repeaters you can hear.</p>
  </div>
</div>
